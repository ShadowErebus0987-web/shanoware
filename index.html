      About

      Shanoware is the home for every game made by Shano. Pick up where you left off, track your endings and unlocks, and get new games as soon as they drop. Starting with Last Light, a pixel-art horror game where your lantern is the only thing keeping the dark away.

        Controls

        Touch
        Left thumb: the joystick moves you. Right thumb: Use, Run, Pour, and the lantern − and +. Pause sits in the top-left corner.
        Keyboard
        WASD move · Shift run · Space use · F pour · QE lantern · Esc pause
        Games play sideways
        Turn your phone when a game opens.
