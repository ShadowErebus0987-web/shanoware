      Where should the tab bar go?

      Pick the side your thumb reaches best. You can change it later in Settings.

      Controls

      Touch
      Left thumb: the joystick moves you. Right thumb: Use, Run, Pour, and the lantern − and +. Pause sits in the top-left corner.
      Keyboard
      WASD move · Shift run · Space use · F pour · QE lantern · Esc pause
      Games play sideways
      Turn your phone when a game opens.
